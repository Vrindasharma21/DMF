History

This blogging page had been created between Oct - Dec in the year 2024. There possibly exists no explanation for what took it so long to appear considering the extremely basic stuff I learnt to make this - safe to say the creator is an engineer. (Pop Up : I love biases that make me look intelligent. Interesting to note that biases are often subtle. Subtly being anything good is even better. I am a Brahman of the best breed by the way.) The colour scheme is pappe - approved. Why did pappe not approve the others (pop-up) is another “ye raaz bhi unhi ke saath chola gaya”. The DMF icon of a beautiful girl with rosy cheeks and bunny teeth is a vague expression of a conflict between a lazy-spring-sunshine mood caged in the confines of an LED library. Or just me explaining a story about myself to a friend.

Moving onto the explainable things now -

  • The Title : DMF as must be apparent from the yellow subs is a nemonic for three expressions namely, Duh (Bebo’s favourite word for below average), Mid (a genZ term for mediocrity) and Fab (short form for fabulous as was devised during the Facebook era). DMF was supposed to be just a review page initially and hence the title was aptly invented. Now, it remains a misnomer for this all rounding blogging page.
  • Personal blogs : This section can contain anything from a torn page of my journal to 20 torn pages of my journal. The idea is to keep it descriptive and real. To tell you about the shit I took during a wedding to the songs I heard before I slept. Basically, I want to talk. (It’s a good film by the way, just that Abhishek doesn’t look Bengali enough.)
  • Reviews : Now a humble request is to not take this part seriously. While you aren’t supposed to take most parts of this page seriously, I thought of communicating again since I have been reported to have a bad sarcasm. This will majorly contain reviews of books and movies, not necessarily recent, in an extremely useless way aimed at boxing them in one of the three titular categories.
  • Useful blogs : Now that’s some responsibility. Here I will just try to share my perspectives on pressing concerns. Something like “top 5 sad songs you can dance to” to “how to reduce your friendships by 80%” but nothing like “MTH-101-101” or “parametric approach to density approximation”. I successfully tried and failed at being left brain useful. I quit.
