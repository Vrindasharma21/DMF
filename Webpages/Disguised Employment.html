Disguised Employment

I left college on 16/05 and ever since I have been waiting for my job location and joining date to appear. In fact, such a blog was never to have existed in the first place. I had thought that blogging would help me vent out my employment frustrations, in case I didn’t have anyone to talk to. Turns out, now I have no-one to talk because everyone has joined and nothing frustrating to vent out even !!!

There was once a certain Vilas in the village Palampur from my economics textbook of class 9th. He would go to work in the fields even when he wasn’t needed. He was unemployed, but in disguise. I do not go to any field but I am assured I will, one day. I have employment but disguised in unemployment, I guess?

My days of disguised employment are characterised by lethal levels of idleness. While this isn’t much of a concern to me, it bothers my family quite a lot. We already have grave trust issues. On top of that, I am a BS graduate from an engineering institute, supposed to work at a consulting firm. They find hard giving importance to my degree or believing I have a job or trusting I haven’t resigned. They are disturbed while I am not. This disturbs them further. As a responsible elder daughter, I try to ease their worries by trying to be just as bothered as them. So :

I wonder

I wonder about my 4 hour commute
I wonder about the 12 hour workdays,

Of the disguised unemployment called training
Of dinners in office when its raining

Of back pain and watery eyes
Of diplomacy and its endless lies

I wonder and I keep wondering
Of my peace and its plundering…

While, I want to delay work for as long as its possible, I wish to keep intact the disguise. Every morning I scan through the newspaper, selectively looking for layoff news and updates on Trump’s mental health. I, more than anyone, want the global economy to be very healthy and everyone to make lots of money and employ lots of people. This should also mean that the companies should have a lot of work, their HRs should be super busy and they should never find out time for stupid things, like onboarding of new recruits.
