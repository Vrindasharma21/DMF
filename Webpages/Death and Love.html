Death and Love

Life update : My favourite character in War and Peace just died, Jim and Pam started talking to each other once again and I finished two boxes of chocolate in 3 weeks.

This favourite character of mine, Prince Andrey, was an officer in the army. He was smart and worldly ; charming and self-aware ; impassive and calm. He was also a little arrogant and proud, but not vile and bitter. This is such a great premise. I love when people like this, who have it all except an EQ get fucked up by life. Interestingly, he is taught more about life by death than life itself. He serves in the war, escapes death, witnesses the death of his wife and father, accepts death as an inevitability, gets heartbroken, desires death and finally dies.
He juggles in and out of atheism throughout these experiences. When he is depressed as fuck, he is fuckless, doesn’t give two shits about community, family and friends and wants to just build some damned garden. But when, sir meets the love of his life, fifteen years younger to him, he is all loving and caring towards the world again. For the first time in the book, he seems to behave like a human and not a robot. Later, his love dumps him for a guy called Anatole and his dad dies. Now not only is he fuckless again but bitter and vile towards Anatole and anybody who reminds him of this past.

On one random war day, he gets blown up by a bomb. He is face to face with death, is in extreme agony, wailing as he is carried into a camp. But someone is wailing even more loudly than him. On the adjacent bed is a patient whose pain seems sharper than his own to Prince Andrey. He is united with this stranger in this moment of pain, and feels a lot of love for this gentlemen. By the way, who is this gentleman ?
Drumrolls, his name is Anatole !!!
At this point, I was blown away. Andrey, on the other hand, is surprised and delighted to discover his ability to love his enemy. He recalls his borderline nun sister talking about Christian love and the universal brotherly love that binds us all. This is the turning point in Prince Andrey’s life, which is only about 40 pages long from hereon. In a final Bollywood masterstroke, he is also reunited with his lover in his last days. He sees the divine light in his final moments and life’s greatest mysteries are revealed to him as he comes closer to death. He almost renounces life as he realises, “Loving everything and everybody, always sacrificing oneself for the sake of love, meant loving no one person, and not living this earthly life”. And so he dies, fuckless yet again, not loving no one person and this earthly life.

War and Peace has two more such confused, completely human characters and several such disturbing plots running in parallel. Through these, the book talks a lot about death and love. Consequently, I have been thinking a lot about death and love. About the former, to me it seems as if death is a revelation of life. Amongst some other things, reading this book, really made me want to die. It is as if I will find answers to questions I can’t put into words.

About the former, that is love, I fail to understand it fully. The book’s religious take on love disappoints me. Yet from whatever I know about love until now, I love it. Yes, I love love. I hadn’t realised it fully until I came across this line from The Office about Jim and Pam. Jim and Pam were best friends. Then, Jim proposed to Pam and she rejected his proposal and now they have been separated. They are in different cities, they miss each other but they can’t acknowledge this. They are confused, painfully human humans, trying to figure out what is wrong with their lives and in comes this banger talking about the strength of love.
“What is love, anyway? Maybe, it’s supposed to break all the rules. Life is short. When two people find each other, what should stand in their way ?”

And ever since I heard this line, I am so charmed by it. This line makes love seem like an adventure. Love seems to speed right through all the obstacles. Being in love must feel like a lifetime psychedelic trip. It should dilate your pupils and increase your heartbeat. It must be very vibrant. For some it maybe eating 20 chocolates in one hour and for others it maybe eating nothing at all. It seems to make life worth living. Love must get in the way of decay. It seems even like the opposite of death or as Tolstoy puts it, it is love that gets in the way of death…
