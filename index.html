Why the author of ‘why men and women cant be friends’ cant be my friend

n our present age of the surplus, we interact with many ideas and thoughts on a daily basis. It is unreasonable to expect that we are moved by all of them. Yes, human sensitivity maybe on the decline. Yet it is not fully destroyed. There still are ideas that can make one leave behind all the stress, dance through life and love all people. There also are ideas that can make one grab a hockey and beat the shit out of anyone who advertises those ideas. At this peculiar juncture in my life, critiquing is more natural than appreciating. I, personally cant resist talking about ideas that deserve public hockey persecution. The act of reviewing the book ’why men and women cant be friends’ presents itself as one such opportunity.
