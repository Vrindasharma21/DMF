Death and Love

Life update : My favourite character in War and Peace just died, Jim and Pam started talking to each other once again and I finished two boxes of chocolate in 3 weeks.

This favourite character of mine, Prince Andrey, was an officer in the army. He was smart and worldly ; charming and self-aware ; impassive and calm. He was also a little arrogant and proud, but not vile and bitter. This is such a great premise. I love when people like this, who have it all except an EQ get fucked up by life. Interestingly, he is taught more about life by death than life itself. He serves in the war, escapes death, witnesses the death of his wife and father, accepts death as an inevitability, gets heartbroken, desires death and finally dies.
He juggles in and out of atheism throughout these experiences. When he is depressed as fuck, he is fuckless, doesn’t give two shits about community, family and friends and wants to just build some damned garden. But when, sir meets the love of his life, fifteen years younger to him, he is all loving and caring towards the world again. For the first time in the book, he seems to behave like a human and not a robot. Later, his love dumps him for a guy called Anatole and his dad dies. Now not only is he fuckless again but bitter and vile towards Anatole and anybody who reminds him of this past.

On one random war day, he gets blown up by a bomb. He is face to face with death, is in extreme agony, wailing as he is carried into a camp. But someone is wailing even more loudly than him. On the adjacent bed is a patient whose pain seems sharper than his own to Prince Andrey. He is united with this stranger in this moment of pain, and feels a lot of love for this gentlemen.
