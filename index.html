In Spite of the Gods

In spite of the Gods is a book written by a journalist Edward Luce published in 2007. I came across this book while I was blog-hopping. A certain centrist atheist sexist 40 year gentleman had appreciated it. Judging by its cover and title, it seemed impressive, and I ended up ordering it.

The book in no way wishes to talk about the cliche of India being the land of elephants and a philosophical retreat as most foreigners often view it. In fact, it aims to honour its pluralism, democracy and diversity and talk about the multitude of problems we face. The book is divided into 8 chapters.
