bad day, again
Today was my first morning in J8 after having passed out from college. And the end to this day, has been nothing like homely. It started with me waking up at 9 in the morning, after setting continuous alarms from 6. Why 6 ? Nothing, I have this habit of acting productive even if I am not. Striking out “laundry” on the whiteboard is one such symptom. The previous night I had not been allowed to play music or listen to Osho at the request of my brother. Cut to morning of 17th May 2025, a Saturday. I came out, with my yellow bag that had my film theory book and my laptop and my journal and a book I am currently reading. I had barely opened my eyes, and then I found out that my brother had closed the doors of the room. My brother is a JEE aspirant, hence closing doors, speaking slowly, following his requests are all serious protocols. It was as if someone had applied the warm filter to the day. The only difference being it wasn’t warm, but “Delhi May hot”. (This deserves to be an adjective.) I saw my father and my grandmother both reading newspapers, seated in the drawing room. It was morbidly silent. The drawing room doesn’t have an AC and my father being as frugal as a beggar, hadn’t turn on the fans.
I took a bottle of water, and filled it with “unable to satiate your thirst temperature” water. (Another adjective plea) The water was not very hot, neither cold. Just some weird temperature that couldn't do anything to my thirst. Instead made me feel worse - for not being content with the water that I was drinking. Later I shared this with my grandmother and dad also, when asked about how was the day going - first of all, who asks this 30mins into the beginning of the day.
